WHY ROOT & ROOM

Plant care can get surprisingly complicated. How much light is bright indirect? How often is often enough? Why are the leaves suddenly turning yellow?

Root & Room brings those questions back down to earth.

You'll find straightforward plant profiles, practical care guides, troubleshooting help, and simple resources designed to help you understand your plants without getting buried in complicated rules.

Whether you're bringing home your first houseplant or trying to figure out what went wrong with a plant you've had for years, Root & Room is here to make the process a little easier.
