BEFORE YOU BUY

It's what's inside that matters.

A beautiful enclosure is lovely, but a healthy habitat begins with choosing a container that meets your isopods' needs.

01

Floor Space Over Height

Most commonly kept terrestrial isopods spend their time exploring the substrate. Prioritize usable floor space over tall containers.

02

Ventilation That Works

Look for secure ventilation that allows airflow without drying the entire habitat. Requirements vary by species.

03

Room for Substrate

Choose a container deep enough for nutritious substrate, leaf litter, and hiding places without crowding the lid.

04

Secure, Accessible Design

A well-fitting lid helps prevent escapes, while easy access makes feeding, misting, and maintenance simpler.
