HOW MINIATURE WORLDS WORK

A little glass garden. A living balance.

A successful terrarium brings together plants, moisture, light, and a suitable growing environment. Understanding how these elements interact helps you create a miniature landscape that can flourish with the right care.

01

Plants

From delicate mosses and miniature ferns to compact tropical plants, choosing species with compatible growing needs is the foundation of a healthy terrarium.

02

Moisture & Light

Light supports plant growth, while moisture helps maintain suitable growing conditions. Closed terrariums retain humidity, while open designs generally require more frequent watering and greater airflow.

03

Substrate & Decomposers

A suitable growing mix supports plant roots and manages moisture. In bioactive terrariums, springtails and beneficial microorganisms can help break down decaying organic matter and recycle nutrients.

Three interconnected elements. One miniature world.

WHERE CURIOSITY BEGINS

Every little forest starts with a single plant.

You don't need a greenhouse or years of gardening experience to create a beautiful miniature landscape. Start with a few thoughtful choices and let your little world grow.

01
FIRST THINGS FIRST

Choose Your Terrarium Style

Decide whether you want a lush, humidity-loving closed terrarium or an airy open arrangement. Your choice will determine which plants, containers, and growing conditions work best.

↗
02
GATHER YOUR MATERIALS

Pick Your Plants & Supplies

Choose a suitable glass container, compatible plants, an appropriate growing mix, and decorative materials. Moss, stones, and natural wood can help bring your miniature landscape together.

↗
03
BRING IT TO LIFE

Build, Observe & Maintain

Arrange your plants and materials, provide appropriate lighting, and water carefully. Watch how your terrarium responds over time and make small adjustments as needed.
