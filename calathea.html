Plant Library

Calathea

A striking houseplant known for patterned foliage, graceful movement, and a little more personality in its care.

Light Medium to low indirect
Water Keep soil lightly and consistently moist
Care More attention
Pet safety Generally considered pet-friendly

Before You Buy

Is this plant right for your home?

Calatheas are beautiful and rewarding, but they're more particular about their growing conditions than many beginner-friendly houseplants.

Good match if...

You enjoy paying attention to your plants and have a space with gentle light and consistent moisture.

Think twice if...

You frequently forget to water or your home tends to have very dry indoor air.

Worth knowing

Calatheas can react noticeably to changes in moisture, humidity, and water quality, so consistency matters.

Care Basics

Consistency matters more than perfection.

Calatheas tend to do best when their growing conditions stay steady, especially when it comes to moisture, light, and humidity.

01

Keep the soil lightly moist

Check the soil regularly and avoid allowing it to become completely dry for extended periods.

02

Choose gentle indirect light

Medium to low indirect light can work well, while harsh direct sunlight can damage the leaves.

03

Pay attention to humidity

Calatheas generally appreciate more humid conditions, particularly when indoor air becomes dry.

What Usually Goes Wrong

Calathea leaves tend to tell you when something changed.

When the foliage starts looking different, check moisture, humidity, and light before assuming there's a bigger problem.

01

Brown or crispy edges

Dry indoor air, inconsistent moisture, or harsh conditions can contribute to browning along the leaves.

→ 02

Drooping or curling leaves

Check the soil and surrounding conditions. Dryness, low humidity, or sudden changes can affect the foliage.

→ 03

Faded or damaged leaves

Too much direct sunlight can cause fading or scorching, while inconsistent conditions can affect new growth.

→

You enjoy plants with dramatic foliage and don't mind giving a little more attention to moisture, humidity, and changing conditions.
