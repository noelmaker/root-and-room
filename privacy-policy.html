Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit Root & Room and how that information may be used.

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Information We Collect

Root & Room does not currently require visitors to create an account or provide personal information in order to browse the website.

Like many websites, certain technical information may be collected automatically when you visit the site. This may include information such as your browser type, device type, pages visited, referring website, and general usage data.

Analytics

Root & Room may use website analytics services to better understand how visitors use the site. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about website traffic and visitor interactions.

This information helps us understand which pages are useful, identify problems with the website, and improve the overall experience.

Cookies

Root & Room and third-party services used by the website may use cookies or similar technologies. Cookies are small files stored on your device that can help websites remember information or understand how visitors interact with a site.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how some website features work.

Affiliate Links

Root & Room may include affiliate links to products or services we believe may be useful to plant owners. If you make a purchase through an affiliate link, Root & Room may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Affiliate relationships do not change the price you pay. Additional information can be found in our Affiliate Disclosure.

Advertising

Root & Room may display advertising in the future. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, or personalize advertisements.

If advertising services are added to the website, this policy may be updated to provide additional information about those services and the choices available to visitors.

Third-Party Websites

Root & Room may link to websites operated by other companies or organizations. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of third-party websites.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any external websites they choose to visit.

Children's Privacy

Root & Room is a general-audience website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Root & Room grows or as the services used by the website change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when significant changes are made.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact Root & Room at sweetwhimsicalwonders@gmail.com .
