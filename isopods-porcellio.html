THE PORCELLIO COLLECTION

Discover the Porcellio genus.

From the bold markings of Dairy Cow isopods to the impressive size of Porcellio hoffmannseggii, explore the diverse species and varieties found within this fascinating genus. Each has its own appearance, behaviors, and care requirements.

01 / PORCELLIO LAEVIS

Porcellio laevis

Porcellio laevis is a smooth-bodied terrestrial isopod known for its active behavior and adaptability. Hobby varieties commonly associated with this species include Dairy Cow, Milkback, and Orange. These isopods are popular among keepers for their interesting patterns, feeding behavior, and colony growth.

POPULAR HOBBY VARIETIES
Dairy Cow Milkback Orange

02 / PORCELLIO SCABER

Porcellio scaber

Commonly known as the Rough Woodlouse, Porcellio scaber is a hardy and adaptable terrestrial isopod recognized for its textured body. Its wide range of colors and patterns has made it especially popular among isopod enthusiasts.

POPULAR HOBBY VARIETIES
Lava Dalmatian Calico Orange
03 / PORCELLIO HOFFMANNSEGGII

Porcellio hoffmannseggii

Commonly known as the Titan Isopod, Porcellio hoffmannseggii is one of the larger terrestrial isopods kept by hobbyists. Recognized for its elongated body, impressive antennae, and active behavior, this species is particularly striking in naturalistic enclosures. It requires careful attention to ventilation and moisture balance.

REPORTED COLOR FORMS & HOBBY VARIANTS
Wild Type Orange Black Chocolate White-Out
04 / PORCELLIO ORNATUS

Porcellio ornatus

Porcellio ornatus is a striking terrestrial isopod appreciated for its contrasting body patterns and distinctive coloration. Different hobby lines display variations in yellow, dark, and pale markings. This species generally prefers good ventilation and a carefully maintained moisture gradient.

COLOR FORMS & HOBBY VARIANTS
High Yellow Dark South
