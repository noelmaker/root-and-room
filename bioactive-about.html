01 / THE STORY

How It All Began

Hello there! My name is Ashley.

I'm the creator of Root & Rot, the bioactive side of Root & Room.

I've always been curious about all kinds of creatures. The creepy crawlies, the scaly ones, the furry ones—you name it! More recently, I stumbled into the fascinating world of isopod keeping and the tiny ecosystems these little creatures call home.

It Started With My Crested Gecko

My journey actually started with a simple idea: I wanted to create a bioactive enclosure for my crested gecko.

I had attempted this several years ago, but no matter what I tried, I couldn't seem to maintain a thriving isopod population in his enclosure. I suspected my gecko might have been snacking on them faster than they could reproduce.

I tried a few different types of isopods over the years, but I could never quite get that balance right.

A Different Approach

So, I came up with another idea. What if I kept a separate breeding colony that I could occasionally take isopods from to replenish my gecko's enclosure?

That way, I'd always have a steady supply without having to constantly purchase more.

And that's how my first Dairy Cow isopod colony came about!

Not Quite As Easy As I Thought

Of course, things didn't go quite as smoothly as I'd imagined.

I experienced quite a few setbacks early on, including losing several isopods without really understanding why. It was frustrating, especially when I thought I was doing everything right.

But after plenty of trial and error, a whole lot of research, and some adjustments along the way, I'm happy to say that my Dairy Cow colony is now thriving!

50+

Baby Dairy Cow Isopods!

From a struggling first colony to what I estimate to be over 50 babies and counting. It's been quite the learning experience!

And Then Came Root & Rot

That experience made me realize just how much there is to learn about these tiny creatures and the environments they live in.

It also inspired me to create a space where others could find helpful information without having to piece everything together from a dozen different places.

And so, Root & Rot was born!

My goal is to make this website a useful, approachable resource for reptile keepers, isopod enthusiasts, bioactive hobbyists, and everyone in between.

Whether you're just getting started or you've been keeping these little creatures for years, I hope you'll find something here that helps you along the way.

A Little Note Before You Go

So, that's the long story made a little less long of how Root & Rot came to be!

Thanks for stopping by. I hope you enjoy exploring the site, learn a thing or two, and maybe even discover a new appreciation for the tiny worlds thriving right beneath our feet.
