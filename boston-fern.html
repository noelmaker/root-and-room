Plant Library

Boston Fern

A lush, classic fern with arching fronds that brings a soft, full look to spaces with the right humidity.

Light Medium to bright indirect
Water Keep soil consistently lightly moist
Care More attention
Pet safety Generally considered pet-friendly

Before You Buy

Is this plant right for your home?

Boston Ferns can bring that lush, full look to a room, but they're less forgiving than some of our other starter plants and prefer consistent moisture and humidity.

Good match if...

You enjoy tending to your plants and have a space with good humidity and indirect light.

Think twice if...

You frequently forget to water or your home tends to be very dry.

Worth knowing

Boston Ferns can react quickly when they become too dry, so consistency matters more than perfection.

Care Basics

Consistency is the secret.

Boston Ferns are happiest when their growing conditions stay relatively consistent, especially when it comes to moisture and humidity.

01

Keep the soil lightly moist

Check the soil regularly and avoid allowing it to become completely dry for extended periods.

02

Give it bright, indirect light

A bright spot away from harsh direct sunlight can provide the conditions it needs for healthy growth.

03

Pay attention to humidity

Boston Ferns generally appreciate humid conditions, particularly when indoor air becomes dry.

What Usually Goes Wrong

Dry conditions tend to show up quickly.

When a Boston Fern starts looking rough, check moisture, humidity, and light before assuming something more serious is happening.

01

Brown or crispy fronds

Dry soil or dry indoor air can cause the fronds and leaflets to turn brown and crisp.

→ 02

Yellowing fronds

Check the soil and watering routine. Both inconsistent moisture and overly wet conditions can cause problems.

→ 03

Drooping or sparse growth

Low humidity, inconsistent moisture, or unsuitable light can affect the plant's overall appearance.
