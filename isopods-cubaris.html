THE CUBARIS COLLECTION

Meet the many faces of Cubaris.

Not all Cubaris are alike. Some have been selectively bred for their colors, while others are known for patterns found in the wild. Take a closer look at the species and hobby varieties, what makes them different, and what they need to thrive.

01 / CUBARIS MURINA

Cubaris murina

Commonly known as the Little Sea isopod, Cubaris murina is a small, adaptable terrestrial isopod found in tropical and subtropical regions. Known for its rounded body and ability to roll into a ball, this species has become popular among hobbyists for its interesting behavior and colorful varieties.

POPULAR HOBBY VARIETIES
Papaya Glacier Anemone Florida Orange
02 / CUBARIS SP. RUBBER DUCKY

Rubber Ducky

Commonly sold as Cubaris sp. 'Rubber Ducky', this distinctive isopod is recognized for its yellow facial markings and rounded body. Popular among collectors, Rubber Duckies are generally slower breeding than many beginner-friendly isopods and benefit from stable, carefully maintained conditions.

DISTINCTIVE CHARACTERISTICS
Yellow Face Slow Breeding Moisture-Loving
03 / CUBARIS SP. CAPPUCCINO

Cappuccino

Commonly sold as Cubaris sp. 'Cappuccino', this attractive terrestrial isopod is known for its warm brown, cream, and tan coloration. Its distinctive patterns and rounded appearance have made it a sought-after addition to isopod collections. Like many tropical hobby isopods, it benefits from stable enclosure conditions and careful moisture management.

DISTINCTIVE CHARACTERISTICS
Coffee-Toned Rounded Body Tropical
04 / CUBARIS SP. PANDA KING

Panda King

Commonly sold as Cubaris sp. 'Panda King', this small terrestrial isopod is recognized for its contrasting dark and pale body markings. Popular among hobbyists for its distinctive appearance and burrowing behavior, Panda Kings can establish thriving colonies when provided with stable humidity, suitable substrate, and plenty of hiding places.

DISTINCTIVE CHARACTERISTICS
Black & White Burrowing Tropical
