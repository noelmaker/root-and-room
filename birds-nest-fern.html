Plant Library

Birds Nest Fern

A lush, sculptural fern with broad, gently arching fronds that brings a soft tropical feel to indoor spaces.

Light Medium to low indirect
Water Keep soil lightly and consistently moist
Care Moderate attention
Pet safety Generally considered pet-friendly

Before You Buy

Is this plant right for your home?

Bird's Nest Ferns bring a lush, sculptural look without needing the floor space of a large tropical plant. They do, however, prefer consistent moisture and gentle conditions.

Good match if...

You want a leafy, sculptural plant for a room with gentle indirect light and don't mind regular watering.

Think twice if...

You frequently forget to water or your space gets strong, direct sunlight for long periods.

Worth knowing

Watering directly into the center of the plant can contribute to problems, so aim the water toward the soil.

Care Basics

Keep it moist, gentle, and undisturbed.

Bird's Nest Ferns appreciate consistent moisture, gentle light, and a little care around the center where their newest fronds emerge.

01

Keep the soil lightly moist

Check the soil regularly and avoid allowing it to become completely dry for extended periods.

02

Choose gentle indirect light

Medium to low indirect light can work well, while harsh direct sunlight can damage the foliage.

03

Protect the center

New growth emerges from the central rosette, so avoid repeatedly pouring water directly into it.

What Usually Goes Wrong

When the fronds change, check moisture and light first.

Bird's Nest Ferns can react noticeably when their growing conditions change, especially when moisture or light is inconsistent.

01

Brown or crispy edges

Dry soil, dry indoor air, or too much direct sunlight can contribute to browning along the fronds.

→ 02

Yellowing fronds

Check the soil and watering routine. Both inconsistent moisture and overly wet conditions can cause problems.

→ 03

Damaged new growth

Changes in moisture, light, or accidental damage around the central rosette can affect emerging fronds.

→

Good Match For

This might be your plant if...

You want a lush, sculptural houseplant that stays relatively contained and you don't mind giving it consistent moisture and a little extra attention.
