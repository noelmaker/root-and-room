Plant Library

Snake Plant

A sturdy, architectural houseplant known for its upright growth and forgiving nature.

Light Bright to low
Water Let soil dry between watering
Care Very little attention
Pet safety Not pet-friendly

Before You Buy

Is this plant right for your home?

Snake Plants are wonderfully forgiving, but every plant has a few things worth knowing before you bring it home.

Good match if...

You want an architectural plant that doesn't need constant attention.

Think twice if...

You have pets that may chew on your houseplants.

Worth knowing

More light can support stronger growth, but this plant can tolerate lower-light spaces.

Care Basics

Keep it happy without making it complicated.

Snake Plants are generally forgiving, but a few simple habits will help them stay healthy and grow well.

01

Let the soil dry

Allow the soil to dry between waterings rather than keeping it consistently wet.

02

Give it good light

It can tolerate lower light, while brighter conditions can support stronger growth.

03

Don't overdo the attention

This is a plant that generally prefers being left alone over constant fussing.

What Usually Goes Wrong

Most problems have a pretty simple cause.

When something looks off, start with the basics. Light, water, and soil are usually the first places to look.

01

Soft or yellow leaves

Often a sign that the soil is staying too wet.

→ 02

Wrinkled leaves

The plant may be ready for a drink.

→ 03

Slow growth

Low light can contribute to slower growth.
