Plant Library

Chinese Money Plant

A compact houseplant with cheerful, rounded leaves that brings a playful shape to shelves, desks, and windowsills.

Light Bright to medium indirect
Water Let soil dry slightly between watering
Care Moderate attention
Pet safety Generally considered pet-friendly

Before You Buy

Is this plant right for your home?

Chinese Money Plants stay relatively compact and bring a playful, rounded shape to shelves, desks, and windowsills. They're a good option when you want personality without a huge footprint.

Good match if...

You want a compact plant with distinctive foliage that works well in smaller spaces.

Think twice if...

Your space has very little natural light or you're looking for a large, dramatic floor plant.

Worth knowing

Chinese Money Plants can produce new offsets around the base, which can eventually be separated and grown on their own.

Care Basics

Give it good light and don't keep it soggy.

Chinese Money Plants are fairly straightforward to care for. Consistent light, sensible watering, and good drainage go a long way.

01

Let the soil dry slightly

Allow the upper portion of the soil to dry before watering again rather than keeping it constantly wet.

02

Give it bright, indirect light

Bright indirect light can support compact, healthy growth and help the plant maintain its shape.

03

Rotate it occasionally

Turning the pot every so often can help the plant grow more evenly toward its light source.

What Usually Goes Wrong

When the leaves change, check the light and soil first.

Chinese Money Plants can show stress through changes in their leaves, especially when watering or light shifts.

01

Yellowing leaves

Check the soil and watering routine. Consistently wet conditions can contribute to yellowing.

→ 02

Drooping leaves

Check the soil before watering. Both very dry and overly wet conditions can cause stress.

→ 03

Leggy growth

Long gaps between leaves can be a sign that the plant isn't getting enough light.
