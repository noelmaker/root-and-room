Plant Library

Monstera

A bold, tropical-looking houseplant with large leaves, dramatic growth, and plenty of presence in a room.

Light Bright to medium indirect
Water Let soil dry between watering
Care Moderate attention
Pet safety Not pet-friendly

Before You Buy

Is this plant right for your home?

Monsteras are rewarding statement plants, but they can eventually become quite large. Give some thought to the space you have now and the space you'll want later.

Good match if...

You want a bold, leafy plant that can become a focal point in a bright room.

Think twice if...

You have very little room or want a plant that will stay small and compact.

Worth knowing

Monsteras can climb and spread as they mature, so a support or extra floor space may eventually help.

Care Basics

Give it good light and room to grow.

Monsteras are fairly adaptable, but consistent light, sensible watering, and enough space will help them develop stronger, healthier growth.

01

Let the soil dry between waterings

Allow the soil to dry somewhat before watering again rather than keeping it constantly damp.

02

Give it bright, indirect light

Bright indirect light can support strong growth and help the plant develop larger, healthier leaves.

03

Give it something to climb

As a Monstera gets larger, a moss pole, trellis, or other sturdy support can help guide its growth.

What Usually Goes Wrong

When the leaves change, check the basics first.

Monsteras can show changes in their leaves when light, watering, or growing conditions aren't quite right.

01

Yellowing leaves

Check the soil and watering routine. Consistently wet conditions can contribute to yellowing.

→ 02

Brown or crispy edges

Dry soil, dry indoor air, or harsh conditions can contribute to browning along the leaves.

→ 03

Small or split-free leaves

Younger plants naturally produce smaller leaves, while insufficient light can limit mature growth.
